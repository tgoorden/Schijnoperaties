Inscribed in a cartouche at the top: Also stetts in der Welt 

Soll man ein Schw(ein) a(u)ffs Küssi setzen Und hie durch alle Künst verletzen Das wär ein Norheit ni(ch)t gar klein Jedoch ist's jetz der Welt gar g(e)mein. (translation: To put a pig on a cushion and therefore injure all the Arts, were no small folly, and yet it is common enough in the world.)

Was nütz das ich ri(e)l bücher hett Wijil alle sach a(u)ff gaaben sthett (translation: Of what use are so many books, or Learning, when all things are controlled by Patronage?)
